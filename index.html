Help and checklist


How is my data protected?
A key is derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2, 250,000 rounds, random salt). Each file and its name are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are saved in this browser's database. Without the passphrase they are unreadable.


Where are my files stored?
In this browser's IndexedDB on this device. Use Export encrypted backup to move them elsewhere; the backup stays encrypted.


I forgot my passphrase
It cannot be recovered. That is the zero-knowledge guarantee. Erase everything and start a new vault.


Shortcuts
/ focuses the AI chat. L locks the vault. The bar at the top jumps between sections. The vault also auto-locks when idle.


Security checklist
Use 16+ random characters. Register a passkey. Export a backup after big changes. Keep your device updated. Never share your Groq key.
